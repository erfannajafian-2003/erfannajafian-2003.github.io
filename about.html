About Me

I am a Master's student in Physics at Sharif University of Technology, specializing in High Energy Physics.

My primary research interest is Quantum Gravity. I am interested in the fundamental nature of spacetime and gravitational phenomena, with broader interests in black hole physics, general relativity, cosmology, and mathematical physics.

My current Master's research focuses on the application of the Method of Regions in Quantum Gravity, under the supervision of Dr. Arash Ardehali.

I completed my Bachelor's degree in Physics at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, where I worked on a research project on the thermodynamics of black holes under the supervision of Dr. Ahmad Ghodsi.
