About Me

I am a graduate student in Physics at Sharif University of Technology, specializing in High Energy Physics.

My primary academic interest is quantum gravity, with broader interests in black hole physics, general relativity, cosmology, and mathematical physics.

My current research focuses on applications of the Method of Regions in quantum gravity.
