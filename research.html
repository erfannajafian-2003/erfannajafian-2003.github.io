RESEARCH

Research Interests

My academic interests lie in theoretical and mathematical physics, particularly in the fundamental structure of spacetime and gravitational phenomena.

Quantum Gravity

My primary research interest is quantum gravity, focusing on the quantum nature of spacetime and gravitational phenomena.

Cosmology

Early-universe cosmology, particularly the pre-inflationary era and quantum fluctuations.

Black Hole Physics

Black hole singularities and the microscopic origin of black hole entropy, including microstate counting in string theory and quantum gravity.

General Relativity and Beyond

Gravitational theories beyond standard general relativity, including physics in two and three spacetime dimensions.

Mathematical Physics

Differential geometry, topology, and their applications in theoretical physics.
