01 / ABOUT

About Me

I am a physics student at Sharif University of Technology, interested in the mathematical and conceptual foundations of theoretical physics.

My academic interests involve the study of fundamental physical theories through mathematical structures, geometry, symmetry, and advanced analytical methods.

This website serves as a platform for presenting my academic background, research interests, publications, and scientific activities.

Research Interests

Quantum Gravity

My primary research interest is quantum gravity, with a focus on understanding the quantum nature of spacetime and gravitational phenomena.

Cosmology

Early-universe cosmology, particularly the pre-inflationary era and the role of quantum fluctuations in the evolution of the early universe.

Black Hole Physics

Black hole singularities and the microscopic interpretation of black hole entropy through microstate counting.

General Relativity and Beyond

Theories and models beyond standard general relativity, including gravitational physics in two and three spacetime dimensions.

Mathematical Physics

Mathematical structures and methods relevant to theoretical physics and the study of fundamental physical theories.
